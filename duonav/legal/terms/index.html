1. Safety first

  • Watch the road, not the phone. Set your route before you start moving. Don't type, search or change settings while driving; pull over, or let a passenger do it.
  • Obey the law and the road. Road signs, signals, closures, police and the conditions in front of you always come before duoNav's directions. If a direction looks unsafe, illegal or impossible, don't follow it.
  • Mount the phone where it doesn't block your view, and follow local laws on using a phone while driving.
  • Speed limits shown are a guide. They come from map data and can be missing, out of date or wrong. The posted limit is what counts.
  • Walking and cycling routes may include roads without pavements, cycle lanes or lighting. Use your own judgement.

2. Map data and routes can be wrong

duoNav's maps, addresses, speed limits and lanes come from OpenStreetMap, a map anyone can edit, and from the routing and search services described in the privacy policy. Traffic information is an estimate. Roads change, and data can be incomplete, out of date or wrong. Routes and arrival times are estimates, not promises.

Downloaded areas reflect the map at the time they were built and update about monthly.
