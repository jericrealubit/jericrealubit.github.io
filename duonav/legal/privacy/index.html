duoNav privacy policy

Effective date: 21 October 2026

duoNav is a map and turn-by-turn navigation app for Android, made by Jeric Realubit ("we"). This policy says what duoNav does with your information. It covers the duoNav app, including the test builds shared before its first release. The short version: there's no account, no ads and no tracking, and almost everything stays on your phone.

What stays on your phone

These are stored only on your device, and we never receive them:

  • Saved places: Home, Work, Church, School and places you star.
  • Recent searches.
  • Your car's location, saved at the end of each drive for Find my car.
  • Settings, such as voice, units, map look and Offline only.
  • Downloaded areas: maps, routing data and search data for the regions you download.

duoNav turns off Android's app backup, so none of this goes to your Google account backup. Deleting duoNav, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, deletes all of it. Forget in Settings › Your car removes the saved car location straight away.

Your location

duoNav asks for location only while you're using it. It uses your location to show where you are, to plan routes from where you are, and to guide you.

  • While you navigate, duoNav keeps guiding you with the screen off by running a visible navigation notification (a foreground service). It never asks for, and never uses, location access "all the time", and it stops using location when navigation ends and the app is closed.
  • Your location history is not recorded or kept. The only location saved is your car's last parking spot, on your phone, as described above.

What leaves your phone, and why

duoNav uses a few services to work online. Each gets only what that request needs:

Your device's IP address, as with any internet request.

These providers process this information to answer the request, under their own privacy policies: Stadia Maps, Protomaps, OpenStreetMap Foundation (overpass-api.de), GitHub, Expo, Google. We don't receive a copy of your requests, and we don't combine them with anything else.

Inside a downloaded area with no signal, routes, search and the map come from your phone and nothing is sent. Offline only (Settings › Privacy and offline) stops all of the requests above except area downloads you start yourself; routes then come from your phone, without traffic.

What we don't do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising, and no advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.
  • We don't sell, rent or share your information with anyone for their own use.
  • No contacts, photos, microphone or camera access.

Children

duoNav isn't aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. Since we collect nothing about any user, there's nothing to delete for a child either; uninstalling removes what's on the phone.

Your choices and rights

  • Location: allow or deny it in Android Settings › Apps › duoNav › Permissions at any time. The map still works without it; navigation doesn't.
  • Offline only: keeps everything on your phone, apart from downloads you start.
  • Delete everything: uninstall duoNav, or Android Settings › Apps › duoNav › Storage › Clear storage.

Depending on where you live (for example under the Australian Privacy Principles, the GDPR in Europe, or privacy laws in your country), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data a service holds about you. We don't hold any about you; the services above handle requests about their own records under their policies. You can still contact us with any question.

Changes

If duoNav starts doing something new with your information (for example, adding purchases), we'll update this policy first and change the effective date above. Changes that matter will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
