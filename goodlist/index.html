What it does

Goodlist is a personal to-do list with alarms that ring until you answer them and medicine reminders you won't miss. It starts simple, grows with you, and speaks 12 languages.

Sign up and start adding tasks in seconds — no group, no setup. Every task you create is private to you by default.

Type a task, or tap the microphone and just say it: "Buy milk tomorrow" becomes a task, due tomorrow. You see the words it heard before anything is saved, and nothing is ever recorded — your phone turns speech into text, and Goodlist only receives the text.

Give a task a day and it turns up on the calendar. Turn on its alarm and your phone rings at the exact time — and keeps ringing until you tap Stop, Snooze or Done. Say "remind me to call the vet at 5pm" and the alarm is already on.

Add a medicine and Goodlist reminds you at every dose, until you answer it. Give it different times on different days if you need to — say 4:10 on weekdays and 9:00 at weekends. Mark each dose taken or skipped with one tap, and see the week's adherence at a glance. It's a reminder and a record, not medical advice.

Tell a pill from a chore without looking: medicine reminders and task alarms ring with different sounds. Pick each one in Settings — Beeps, Chime, Bell or Rising — and hear a preview first.

Goodlist speaks your language: English, Español, Português, Français, Deutsch, Русский, العربية, हिन्दी, Bahasa Indonesia, Filipino, 日本語 and 简体中文. It follows your phone, or you can pick one in Settings. Arabic lays out every screen right to left.

When you're ready, create or join a group — Family or Team — with an invite code, and send each other Requested tasks with your name on them, so it's always clear who asked for what. Family on an iPhone or a computer can join at goodlist.expo.app. Your personal tasks stay exactly as they were.

Price and privacy

Goodlist is free. Every new account gets 30 days of Premium, no card needed. Premium — monthly, yearly or a one-time Lifetime purchase — adds smart lists, My stats, remind-me-before, a printable medicine record, alarm sounds from your phone and Done buttons on the widget; for groups, a second group, sharing a medicine and up to 20 members. Questions

Is Goodlist free?

Yes. Your own tasks, alarms, medicine reminders and one group of up to 5 people are free, with no ads. Premium is optional: it adds smart lists, My stats, a printable medicine record and more for you, and a second group, sharing a medicine and groups of up to 20 for your group. Every new account gets 30 days of Premium, no card needed.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not yet. Family and teammates on an iPhone or a computer can use Goodlist at goodlist.expo.app with the same account: they can join your group, and see and send tasks. Alarms and medicine reminders need the Android app, because a browser can't schedule them.

What if an alarm doesn’t ring?

Check that Silent mode is off in Settings. Then open the Calendar: if a phone setting would stop alarms, a box at the top says so, with a button to fix it. Goodlist needs permission to send notifications, and Android’s “Alarms & reminders” turned on. Some phones also stop apps in the background to save battery; if yours does, let Goodlist run without battery restrictions.

How do I share tasks with my family?

Create a group, Family or Team, in the Group tab, then tap Share on its card to send the invite. Once they’ve joined, you can send each other Requested tasks, each with the name of the person who asked. Your personal tasks stay private.

What happens to my data?

Goodlist doesn’t show ads, doesn’t track you for advertising, and doesn’t sell your data. You can delete your account and all of your data at any time, right from Settings. The privacy policy has the details.
