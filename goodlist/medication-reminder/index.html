A medication reminder that keeps ringing until you answer

Goodlist reminds you at the times you set for each medicine — as many medicines as you take, on the free plan — and keeps ringing until you answer it.

Get it on Google Play See everything Goodlist does

For Android phones. On an iPhone or a computer, use the web app at goodlist.expo.app with the same account. Alarms and reminders need the Android app.

Free, with no limit on medicines

  • Any number of medicines, each with its own times and dose
  • Every day, some days, or different times on different days
  • Reminders that ring until you answer, even with the phone locked
  • Taken or Snooze straight from the notification, Skip in the app
  • A record of every dose, and how many you took this week
  • Your to-do list and alarms in the same app, with no ads

For family and carers

Share a medicine with a family group and they see each dose as taken, skipped or missed — useful when you help a parent from another town or another country. If a shared dose still isn’t logged an hour after its time, the group gets an alert.

Sharing is part of Premium. Every new account starts with 30 days of it, with no card needed and nothing charged when it ends. A group of up to 5 people is free.

Set it up in a minute

  1. Install Goodlist from Google Play and continue with Google.
  2. Open Meds, tap +, and add the medicine with its times.
  3. Allow notifications, and Android’s “Alarms & reminders”, so it rings on the minute.

Good to know

Goodlist reminds you at the times you choose; it doesn’t give medical advice. Take your medicines as your doctor or pharmacist tells you.

Some phones stop apps in the background to save battery. If a reminder doesn’t ring, Settings → Help → Alarm check shows the steps for your phone.

Questions

Are medicine reminders free?

Yes. Reminders, any number of medicines and the record of every dose are free, with no ads. Premium adds extras such as sharing a medicine with family and a printable medicine record.

Does it work on an iPhone?

Reminders ring in the Android app. Family on an iPhone or a computer can use Goodlist at goodlist.expo.app with their own account to see a medicine you share with them.

Who can see my medicines?

Only you, unless you choose to share a medicine with a group. Goodlist doesn’t show ads, doesn’t track you for advertising and doesn’t sell your data, and you can delete your account at any time.
